🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026  ·  v1.1

VEYRONIS ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered student assistant platform (the "Service").

By using VEYRONIS, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our Service:

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address – used for login, account recovery, and important notifications.
  • Password – securely hashed using bcrypt with SHA256 pre‑hashing. We never store plain‑text passwords.
  • Name – optional, used for personalisation (e.g., "Welcome back, [Name]").
  • Profile picture – optional, stored securely via Cloudinary.
  • Display ID – a unique, user‑friendly identifier (e.g., VY‑A1B2C3) generated for each account.
  • Ban status and reason – if you violate our Terms, we may store a ban record with the reason and expiration date.

1.2 Usage Data

  • Messages & conversations – all text you send to VEYRONIS, including AI responses, are stored to maintain conversation context and improve the service.
  • Uploaded files – images, documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, CSV, Excel) are temporarily processed and stored securely.
  • AI interactions – metadata such as model used, response time, and token usage.
  • Hindsight simulations – scenarios and simulation results are stored to provide context and improve the feature.

1.3 Technical Data

  • IP address – used for rate limiting, security, and analytics.
  • Browser type and version – to ensure compatibility and improve performance.
  • Device information – operating system, screen resolution, and network type.
  • Usage patterns – how you interact with the Service (feature usage, session duration, etc.).

1.4 Voice Data (if enabled)

  • Voice recordings – processed in real‑time using browser‑based speech recognition. Audio is not stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service – generate AI responses, process uploads, and manage your account.
  • To improve and personalise your experience – remember your preferences, response style, and conversation history.
  • To communicate with you – send verification emails, password resets, security alerts, and important service updates.
  • To monitor usage and ensure security – detect and prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and protect against unauthorised access.
  • To analyse and improve the Service – understand usage patterns, fix bugs, and optimise performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations – as required by applicable laws and regulations.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take data security seriously and implement industry‑standard measures to protect your information.

  • Encryption – all data transmissions are encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Hashing – passwords are hashed using bcrypt with SHA256 pre‑hashing. We never store plain‑text passwords.
  • Secure hosting – data is stored on Render.com servers with industry‑standard security measures.
  • File storage – uploaded files are stored securely via Cloudinary, with access controlled by user authentication.
  • Database – PostgreSQL with parameterised queries to prevent SQL injection.
  • Access control – strict ownership checks ensure users can only access their own data.

While we take all reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access – you can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction – you can update or correct your personal information at any time.
  • Deletion – you can permanently delete your account and all associated data via the "Delete Account" option in Settings.
  • Export – you can export your conversations in JSON or TXT format using the "Share" feature.
  • Restriction – you can request that we limit the processing of your data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Objection – you can object to certain types of processing, such as analytics.
  • Withdraw consent – you can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at mishobazadze@gmail.com or use the account management features within the app.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After you delete your account, we securely delete all associated data:

  • Account data – deleted immediately upon account deletion.
  • Conversations – deleted with the account.
  • Uploaded files – deleted with the account.
  • Usage logs – retained for up to 30 days for analytics and security, then anonymised or deleted.
  • Email verification tokens – automatically expire after 24 hours.
  • Password reset tokens – automatically expire after 1 hour.
  • Ban records – retained for the duration of the ban, and may be kept for audit purposes after expiry.

We may retain anonymised or aggregated data for analytical purposes after your account is deleted. This data does not personally identify you.

7. Cookies & Tracking

VEYRONIS uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

  • Authentication – JWT tokens stored in localStorage to keep you logged in.
  • Preferences – theme preference, response style, and custom instructions are stored locally.
  • Analytics – we may use aggregated, anonymised analytics to understand usage patterns.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Service.

8. Children's Privacy

VEYRONIS is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.
